    <h2>Research interests</h2>
    <p>My work combines geometry, analysis, and computation to understand the global behavior of nonlinear Hamiltonian systems.</p>
  </div>

    <article class="aa-feature-card">

    </article>
    <article class="aa-feature-card">
      <span class="aa-card-number">02</span>
      <h3>Geometric Reduction</h3>
      <p>Structure-preserving coordinates and singularity-free reductions for systems with symmetry.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="aa-feature-card">

    </article>
