<div class="v2-page-intro">
  <p class="v2-kicker">Research program</p>
  <p class="v2-page-lead">My research lies at the intersection of Hamiltonian dynamical systems, geometric mechanics, bifurcation theory, and mathematical fluid dynamics.</p>
  <p>I develop structure-preserving reductions and global geometric descriptions that reveal how invariant structures, singular configurations, and parameter changes organize nonlinear dynamics.</p>
</div>

<section id="hamiltonian-systems" class="v2-research-block">
  <div class="v2-research-block__number">01</div>
  <div><h2>Hamiltonian Systems</h2><p>I study nonlinear systems whose dynamics are governed by Hamiltonian structure, conserved quantities, and symmetries. A central objective is to identify coordinates and geometric objects that make global dynamics visible without destroying the underlying structure.</p></div>
</section>

<section id="geometric-reduction" class="v2-research-block">
  <div class="v2-research-block__number">02</div>
  <div><h2>Geometric Reduction</h2><p>Reduction transforms high-dimensional systems into lower-dimensional models while preserving essential invariants. My work emphasizes singularity-free formulations that remain valid across parameter regimes and clarify the topology of the reduced phase space.</p></div>
</section>

<section id="bifurcation-theory" class="v2-research-block">
  <div class="v2-research-block__number">03</div>
  <div><h2>Bifurcation Theory</h2><p>I investigate how equilibria, invariant manifolds, and global phase portraits change as physical parameters vary. This leads to bifurcation sets that organize qualitative transitions and provide a unified view of distinct dynamical regimes.</p></div>
</section>

<section id="vortex-dynamics" class="v2-research-block">
  <div class="v2-research-block__number">04</div>
  <div><h2>Vortex Dynamics</h2><p>Point-vortex and vortex-dipole models provide a rich setting where geometry, topology, and fluid mechanics interact. I use these systems to develop reduction techniques and to classify global dynamics, relative equilibria, and bifurcations.</p></div>
</section>
