---

We investigate the global phase-space structure of three interacting point vortices in a two-dimensional inviscid, incompressible fluid. Using Jacobi coordinates followed by Lie–Poisson reduction, we derive a singularity-free reduced system for arbitrary vortex circulations and use it to analyze the global phase portrait and bifurcation structure.
